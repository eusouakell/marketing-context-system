# Example — Task context bundle

## Task

Write an executive article about AI-agent governance for banking CTOs.

## Routed context

### Brand
- approved positioning;
- tone constraints;
- claims policy.

### Audience
- CTO priorities;
- operational concerns;
- buying context.

### Market
- current banking / AI-agent signals;
- geography and time window.

### Evidence
- dated sources;
- claim-to-source mapping.

### Skill
- executive-content.

### Verification
- canonical-source check;
- claim-has-evidence check;
- brand consistency eval;
- human publication gate.

## Excluded context

- unrelated campaigns;
- historical personas;
- vendor material not needed for the article;
- full brand manual when specific canonical concepts suffice.
