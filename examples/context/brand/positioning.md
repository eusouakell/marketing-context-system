# Positioning — canonical

Status: current

Northstar Labs is a fictional strategic technology consultancy for large organizations.

The brand should lead with **decision quality and working capability**, not with access to headcount or generic digital transformation.

Preferred framing:
- make technology decisions with trade-offs visible;
- turn decisions into capabilities teams can use;
- preserve room for future choices.

Avoid:
- "future-proof";
- "revolutionary";
- unsupported speed or ROI claims.
