A banking CTO evaluating agentic AI is likely to care about:

- production reliability;
- integration with existing platforms;
- identity and authorization;

- operating model;

## Executive article policy

# Executive article policy

Audience: senior technology leadership.

Required:
- open with a decision or operating tension;
- distinguish observation from recommendation;
- explain mechanisms and trade-offs;
- use concrete operational consequences;
- avoid unsupported market-size or ROI claims.

Length target for benchmark: 500–700 words.
