# Evidence Grounding

For every material factual or performance claim:

1. identify the claim;
2. identify the evidence ID;
3. check population / geography / date where relevant;
4. check whether the evidence supports the exact scope of the claim;
5. downgrade, qualify or remove unsupported language;
6. preserve provenance for review.

A plausible claim without evidence remains unsupported.
