# Context Audit

Check:

- Does every included item affect the task?
- Is any lower-authority source displacing a canonical source?
- Is superseded material present?
- Are two items duplicating the same concept?
- Is evidence separated from interpretation?
- Could any context be isolated or loaded just in time?
- Is the bundle within its declared token budget?

Output: keep, remove, compress or escalate.
