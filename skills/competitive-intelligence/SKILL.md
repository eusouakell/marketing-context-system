# Competitive Intelligence

## Inputs

- geography;
- ICP;
- competitor universe;
- time window;
- current positioning / offers;
- decision criteria.

## Workflow

1. Gather dated signals.
2. Separate fact from interpretation.
3. Classify the move.
4. Assess relevance to the defined ICP and geography.
5. Compare against current strategy.
6. Produce implications and open questions.
7. Route strategic changes to a human gate.

## Output

For each signal:

- what changed;
- source and date;
- market scope;
- commercial implication;
- relevance;
- follow-up question;
- confidence.
