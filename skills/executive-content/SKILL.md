# Executive Content

## Required context

- audience / buying role;
- brand positioning;
- topic evidence;
- editorial constraints;
- claim rules.

## Method

1. Start from the executive decision or tension.
2. Establish the problem with evidence.
3. Explain mechanism, trade-offs and implications.
4. Separate observation from recommendation.
5. Avoid generic transformation language.
6. Prefer operational consequences and decision criteria.
7. Verify material claims.

## Do not

- invent proof;
- turn vendor copy into the company's point of view;
- make unsupported superiority claims;
- substitute tone for substance.
