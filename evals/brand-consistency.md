# Eval — Brand consistency

Evaluate after deterministic checks pass.

Questions:

1. Does the artifact preserve approved positioning?
2. Is preferred terminology used consistently?
3. Is tone appropriate to the audience?
4. Are new factual or performance claims introduced?
5. Does the writing sound specific rather than generic?
6. Were strategic distinctions flattened or altered?

Result:

`PASS`, `REVISE`, or `ESCALATE`.

The eval may flag strategy; it may not rewrite canonical strategy.
